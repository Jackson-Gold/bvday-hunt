♡ Encrypt a vault link

Lock a Spotify or Google Drive link with your master password so the real URL never appears in the source. Paste the result into the enc field in content.js.

—
How it works
The link is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your master password (PBKDF2, 150k rounds). Only someone who knows the password can decrypt it — and the password is never stored anywhere, only its one-way hash. The encryption happens entirely in your browser.

Important
If you ever change the master password, you must re-encrypt every vault link here with the new password, or they won't open on the site.
